## First results: SEC filings (Nov 3)

I pulled every 8-K and 6-K our 194 companies filed with the SEC in the 14 days to Nov 3 and compared them with Massive's rules-only picks for the same 14 days. Adding filings takes coverage from 74 tickers (38%) to 112 (58%), and an 8-K's item number labels the event without any guessing. Filings fill in 8 of the 23 tickers Massive has no articles for at all.

A few terms used below:

- **8-K:** a form US companies must file with the SEC within four business days of certain events. Each 8-K lists **item numbers** that say what happened, e.g. 2.02 means quarterly results.
- **6-K:** what foreign companies (TSMC, ASML, Nokia, STMicro…) file instead. It has no item numbers; it is usually just a press release.
- **Press release exhibit (EX-99.1):** the company's press release, attached to the filing as a separate document.
- **Earnings season:** the few weeks each quarter when most companies report results. Late October to mid-November is one.

| --- | --- |
| Tickers with a material Massive story (same rules as the Oct 30 run) | 74 of 194 (38%) |
| Tickers with a material 8-K | 93 (48%); 91 by item number alone |
| Tickers with a material 6-K | 9 |

14 of the 38 "filings only" tickers filed in the last 24 hours, mostly earnings released at 4 PM on Nov 3, so Massive may still catch up on some of them.

### What each item number means, and what we show

| 8-K item | What it means | Show? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2.02 | Quarterly results | Show (earnings) |
| 1.01, 1.02, 2.01 | Signed or ended a major agreement (a merger, a big contract, a loan); finished buying or selling a business | Show (deal) |
| 5.02 | An executive or director joined or left, or their pay changed | Show (leadership) |
| 2.03, 3.02, 3.03 | New debt, new shares sold privately, changes to shareholders' rights | Show (capital) |
| 1.03, 1.05, 2.04–2.06, 3.01, 4.01, 4.02 | Warning signs: bankruptcy, a cyberattack, layoffs, a write-down, a delisting notice, a new auditor, restated results | Show (none this window) |
| 7.01, 8.01 | Catch-alls: "here is a press release or investor deck" | Only if the attached press release's headline passes our news rules |
| 9.01 | "Exhibits attached" | Never on its own; it is how we find the press release |
| 5.03, 5.07 and other housekeeping | Bylaw changes, shareholder vote results | Hide |

**The 7.01 / 8.01 rule in practice.** 15 filings had only these items. The rule showed 5: the Axcelis–Veeco merger timing update (filed by both), HIVE's $129M financing, TTM's new buyback and Vertiv's acquisition. It correctly hid 7: a conference-call notice, two investor decks, prospectus and shelf-registration paperwork, an employee stock plan vote, and Microsoft re-issuing the business section of its annual report. It missed 2: Applied Materials raising its stake in BE Semiconductor, and Tempus's new deals with Bristol-Myers Squibb, which came with no press release. Celestica's Toronto buyback approval is borderline.

### Earnings: filings aren't much faster, but they don't miss

There were 81 earnings 8-Ks in the window. Massive had a material earnings story for 55 of them. Its first story (almost always Zacks) arrived a median of 0.9 hours after the filing: 28 within an hour, 43 within six hours, 11 more than 12 hours later. For the other 26, Massive had no earnings story at all. 12 of those were filed in the last 24 hours, but 14 still had nothing a day or more later, including Flex, Entegris, Hubbell and Welltower, which reported a week earlier.

### The 6-K gap

16 of our tickers file 6-Ks: ARM, ASML, ASX, BULL, CCJ, GFS, INMD, KLAR, NBIS, NOK, POET, SIMO, STM, TSEM, TSM and WIX. TSMC and ASML reported results in mid-October, before this window.

- 10 of them filed 15 6-Ks in the window. Six attached the press release as EX-99.1, and the rest pasted it into the 6-K itself.
- I found a headline for all 15, and our news rules would show 13.
- Among these 16 tickers, Massive covers 7, 6-Ks cover 9, and together they cover 11.
- Mistakes: two STMicro share-buyback notices labelled "earnings" (the headline says "results of trading"), and InMode's special-committee letter labelled "leadership". TSMC's monthly insider-holdings report was correctly hidden.

So 6-Ks work if we treat them like news articles: run the same headline rules (or the LLM) on the press release.

### It's earnings season

Over the past year, a typical 14 days had **36** tickers with a newsworthy 8-K (range 17 to 111), against 91 in this window. Leaving out quarterly results, a typical 14 days has about 27 (range 13 to 44). Outside earnings season, then, 8-Ks add roughly 25–35 tickers of news per two weeks: leadership changes, deals and financings.

### What a filing card needs

8-K titles say nothing ("Current report"), so the card has to be built from the filing's parts:

- **Link to the press release, not the filing.** 87% of the 8-Ks we'd show have one (EX-99.1). It is the readable document; the 8-K itself is a legal cover page that says "see Exhibit 99.1". With no press release, link the 8-K document itself, never the filing's index page (a list of files).
- **Use the first press release exhibit (EX-99.1).** Later ones are usually slides, CFO commentary or financial tables; Cameco attached five.
- **Title: the press release headline.** Found for all 99. 21 of 112 headlines are in ALL CAPS and should be converted to normal case. Earnings headlines are generic ("AMD Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results"), so a summary with the key number would help.
- **A one-line summary is required when there is no press release.** That is 15 of 114 8-Ks (13%): leadership changes, loan agreements and debt deals. Their only text is a legal sentence of about 300 characters, e.g. "On October 19, 2026, Richard Rothberg, Executive Vice President, General Counsel and Secretary of Dell Technologies Inc. ('the Company'), notified the Company of his decision to retire…". The planned LLM step can write it. A fallback is a template built from the item, e.g. "Leadership change (8-K item 5.02)".
- **Event label:** from the item number for 8-Ks; from the headline rules for 6-Ks and 7.01/8.01 filings. One filing can carry several items: Marvell's results 8-K attached the merger press release, so its headline and its earnings label disagree.
- **Time:** the SEC's acceptance time, shown in ET.
- **Skip amendments (8-K/A)** when the original is already shown: 4 of the 114 amend an earlier filing.

Mock, using the Oct 21 DELL filing:

```text
▾ DELL
  LEADERSHIP · 8-K  General Counsel Richard Rothberg is retiring  ↗
           SEC filing · Oct 21, 4:37 PM ET
           Rothberg, also Dell's chief legal officer and Secretary, told the company on Oct 19.
```

### Where filings fall short

| Ticker | What happened | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| NVDA, CBRS, MKSI | Oracle partnerships, a factory expansion | Not 8-K events; only news covers these |
| AMAT | "Applied Materials to Increase its Strategic Investment in BE Semiconductor" was hidden | Our news rules only count an investment when it names a $ amount |
| TEM | New deals with Bristol-Myers Squibb were hidden | 8.01 with no press release attached, only legal text |
| MOD, LSCC, AEHR | A severance-plan tweak and committee seats shown as "leadership" | Item 5.02 also covers pay and committee changes |
| MRVL, CTRE | Credit-line amendments shown as "deal" | Item 1.01 also covers loan agreements |

I hand-checked the 25 non-earnings 8-Ks the item numbers would show: 19 were real news (mergers, acquisitions, CFO and general-counsel changes, board seats, financings), and 6 were housekeeping like the cases above.

### What this tells us

- **Filings are the free fix for coverage.** 8-Ks and 6-Ks take us from 74 tickers to 112 (58%), and fill 8 of the 23 tickers Massive never mentions.
- **Item numbers are a free, reliable label.** The misses are housekeeping that a small rule or the LLM step can drop, such as committee seats and credit-line amendments.
- **Not faster, but complete.** When Zacks covers an earnings report it is usually within an hour of the filing, but Massive never covered 26 of the 81.
- **Massive still adds things filings can't.** Partnerships and product news (NVDA, CBRS, MKSI) never appear in an 8-K. But 4 of the 10 "Massive only" picks aren't real news about the company: CRWD and CSCO are stories about other companies, and CRWV and SMR are opinion.
- **It's earnings season.** Expect about 36 tickers a fortnight from 8-Ks, not 91.
- **82 tickers still have nothing, and often that is the true answer.** Of the 15 tickers with no articles and no material filing, 14 filed no 8-K or 6-K at all. Nothing happened that a company must report, and many report results later in November. The paid-feed trial should be judged on whether it finds real news for these.
